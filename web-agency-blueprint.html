Course Overview

The Web Agency Blueprint is a complete step-by-step system for launching and scaling a high-ticket niche web design agency. It moves from zero to $10k+/month through proven systems for niche selection, client acquisition, sales, fulfilment, and outsourcing. All materials are designed to be implemented immediately — no fluff or theory.

PAGES Framework (Referenced in Related Content)

From Jack Roberts' related AntiGravity tutorial — a design operating system for building conversion-focused websites:

  • P — Panel Layout: structure and content hierarchy
  • A — Style & Aesthetic: visual direction and mood
  • G — Color & Theme: palette and branding
  • E — Typography: type scale and pairing
  • S — Animations & Interactions: motion design
