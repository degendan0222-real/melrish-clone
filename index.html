Good to Know

Wedding photography and videography in Dubai, answered

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Dubai?

Wedding photography in Dubai usually runs from around AED 7,000 to AED 18,000 for full-day coverage, with luxury and combined photo-and-film packages priced above that. We build each package around your day, so the most accurate way to get a figure is to send us your date and venue for a tailored quote.

Do you offer both wedding photography and videography?

Yes. We are a full wedding photography and videography team in Dubai. One studio covers your day with fine art photography and cinematic documentary films, including same-day edits, so the look and feel stay consistent across both.

Do you travel for destination weddings outside Dubai?

Yes. We are based in Dubai and shoot across the UAE, with a second studio in Manila, and we travel worldwide for destination weddings. In 17 years we have photographed more than 1,000 weddings across many cultures and countries.

What is a same-day edit wedding video?

A same-day edit is a short cinematic film we edit on-site during your celebration and play back to your guests the same night, usually at the reception. It is one of our signature services and a moment your guests never see coming.

How far in advance should we book?

Dubai's wedding season runs roughly October to April and the best dates book months ahead. If you have a date in mind, reach out early so we can hold it for you.
